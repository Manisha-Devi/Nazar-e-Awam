# Privacy policy

**Route:** `/privacy`

**Eyebrow:** Information care

This policy describes the limits and intended handling of information voluntarily sent to Nazar-e-Awam. The final policy must be reviewed against the actual hosting configuration and applicable law before launch.

## Information sent by email

If you email us, the message may contain your email address, contact information, the details you choose to share and attachments. We use this information to review a submission, assess evidence, seek clarification, consider a response or correction request, and communicate about the matter.

## Private correspondence and publication

Private correspondence is separate from published content and is not automatically published. If a report is considered, we assess whether information can be used safely and fairly. We minimise personal details and may redact identifying information where appropriate.

## Access, retention and deletion

Access to correspondence should be limited to people who need it for review. Information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for that purpose, accountability and applicable legal obligations, then securely deleted where feasible. These practices must be confirmed against actual email and hosting arrangements before launch; this statement is not a claim of secure storage.

## Providers and legal disclosure

Email and hosting providers may process information as part of delivering their services, under their own terms and systems. Information may be disclosed where legally required. We cannot promise that email is end-to-end encrypted, that anonymity is guaranteed, or that correspondence is confidential in every circumstance.

## Your choices and enquiries

Do not send passwords, OTPs, banking credentials, unnecessary identity documents, or classified or highly sensitive information through ordinary email without appropriate guidance. Privacy questions or requests can be sent to **genz@nazar-e-awam.com**.
