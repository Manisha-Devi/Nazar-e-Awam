# Funding disclosure

**Route:** `/funding`

**Eyebrow:** Independence

No donor, sponsor or financial figure is represented here without verified information.

## What we will disclose

When verified funding information exists, it should identify the source and nature of donations or grants, relevant conditions, and operating expenses at an appropriate level of detail. Records should be documented and disclosed clearly.

## Editorial independence

Funders must not control favourable coverage, determine findings or suppress justified reporting. Funding decisions and editorial decisions should remain separate, with relevant conflicts disclosed and managed.

## Current disclosure

No donors, sponsors, grants or financial figures are published at this time. This is not a claim that the project has no costs or receives no support.
