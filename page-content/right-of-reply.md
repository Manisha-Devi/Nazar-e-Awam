# Right of reply

**Route:** `/right-of-reply`

**Eyebrow:** Fairness

Individuals, departments and institutions may provide a response or factual clarification about a published report or questions raised for a potential report.

## How to send a reply

Please identify the report or correspondence, the specific factual claims you are addressing, any proposed corrections, supporting records and—where applicable—the authorised respondent and their role.

**Link:** Email a right-of-reply request  
**Pre-filled email subject:** Right of Reply — [Report or Issue]

## Review and publication

Responses are reviewed for relevance, authenticity, privacy and editorial standards. We may seek clarification and edit for length without changing meaning. Publication of a response is not automatic. Where a response is published, we aim to connect it to the relevant report and distinguish it from our independent assessment.
