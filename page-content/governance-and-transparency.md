## Principles

- Public-interest mission and evidence-led publication.
- Editorial independence from funders and interested parties.
- Transparent, explainable editorial decision-making.
- Disclosure and management of relevant conflicts of interest.
- Financial accountability and clear separation of funding decisions from editorial decisions.
