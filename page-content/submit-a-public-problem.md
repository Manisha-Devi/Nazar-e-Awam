# Submit a public problem

**Route:** `/submit`

**Eyebrow:** Raise a concern

We consider demonstrable public-interest concerns. Email is the only submission method in this first version; there is no web form or secure upload portal.

## What may be relevant

You may write to us about:

- Public-service problems and delays in official processes.
- Administrative irregularities.
- Public expenditure and service-delivery concerns.
- Questions about public institutions.
- Difficulty accessing public information or seeking accountability.
- Other demonstrable matters of public interest.

## What to include

- A concise description of the problem.
- The department or institution concerned.
- Dates and location, where relevant.
- Supporting documents or public records, if available.
- Specific questions that may be independently checked.
- Whether you request that your identity not be disclosed publicly.
- Optional contact details if you would like follow-up.

> **Before sending:** An email does not guarantee anonymity or confidential handling. Do not send passwords, one-time passwords, banking credentials or unnecessary identity documents. Redact third-party personal information where possible. Do not send classified or highly sensitive information through ordinary email without appropriate guidance.

## What happens next

A submission is reviewed, not automatically published. An allegation is not automatically a verified fact. We may request clarification, check available evidence and contact the relevant authority for a response. Private correspondence is not automatically published. We do not guarantee coverage, action by an authority or a particular outcome.

Ordinary email and the email and hosting providers involved have limits. Please consider the information and risks before writing.
