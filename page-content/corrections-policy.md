# Corrections policy

**Route:** `/corrections`

**Eyebrow:** Accountability

We take substantiated reports of factual errors seriously and make significant corrections visible.

## Report a possible error

Tell us which report and passage is at issue, what you believe is inaccurate, what correction you propose and any supporting records. Affected parties may request review.

**Link:** Email a correction request  
**Pre-filled email subject:** Correction Request — [Report Title]

## Review and record

We recheck factual claims against available evidence and may ask follow-up questions. If a material error is confirmed, we correct it clearly and identify significant changes rather than silently hiding them. Dates and relevant updates are recorded on the report where appropriate. A request alone does not establish an error, and not every disagreement requires a correction.
