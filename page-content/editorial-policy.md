# Editorial policy

**Route:** `/editorial-policy`

**Eyebrow:** Standards

Our editorial decisions are based on evidence, fairness and public interest—not political affiliation, personal influence or payment.

## Accuracy and evidence

We check claims against records and independent sources where possible. A document submitted to us is not automatically authentic. Reports distinguish allegations, evidence, verified facts, official statements and editorial conclusions.

## Public interest and independence

Publication must serve a clear public-interest purpose. Editorial decisions are not for sale and are not based on political affiliation, personal influence or payment. We do not accept payment for favourable coverage. Actual conflicts of interest should be identified and addressed before publication.

## Fairness and right of reply

We make reasonable efforts to put material factual questions to the people or institutions concerned and fairly represent relevant responses. A response is subject to verification, privacy and editorial review.

## Privacy and source protection

We minimise unnecessary personal information and assess the risks of identifying people. We take care with source information, while being honest that ordinary email cannot guarantee anonymity or confidentiality.

## Sensitive information and legal risk

Editors assess whether publication could cause avoidable harm, disclose private or protected information, compromise safety, or create legal risk. We redact details that are not necessary to explain the public issue.

## Corrections and accountability

Significant factual corrections are made transparently rather than silently hidden. We record relevant update and correction dates and invite substantiated correction requests.
