## Why this exists

# A careful process for questions that matter.

People deserve a fair way to raise demonstrable public concerns. We assess evidence, seek the relevant authority’s account and publish only when the facts and public interest justify it.

## Our method

# Four steps. No shortcuts.

Every concern is reviewed. A submission is not a finding, and publication is never promised.

### 01 — Receive the concern

We screen for public interest, clarity and safe handling.

### 02 — Verify the evidence

We check records, dates and independent corroboration.

### 03 — Seek the official response

Relevant people and institutions have a fair opportunity to reply.

### 04 — Publish responsibly

Editors weigh evidence, privacy, fairness and public value.

## Care with information

# Privacy is not a promise we can overstate.

Email is the only submission route in this first version. Ordinary email cannot guarantee anonymity or secure handling.

> **Before sending:** Please do not send passwords, one-time codes, banking credentials, unnecessary identity documents, or classified and highly sensitive material through ordinary email. Redact third-party personal information where possible.
